Terra × Terracotta

Terra’s calm, Terracotta’s warmth — one design.

The team vote split: the majority chose Terra, two members held out for Terracotta. This hybrid is the peace treaty. It keeps Terra’s editorial serif, warm paper and wheat-field light exactly as the majority voted — and lets Terracotta contribute the two things its supporters loved most: the clay accent colour and the arch, used as a quiet structural motif instead of decoration. Rule of the blend: a tight palette, no noise — paper, ink and one clay accent, with field green kept for crop status alone and gold only in the sun medallion.

01 · TERRA — MAJORITY PICK

What Terra keeps

The foundation. Nothing the majority voted for is diluted.

  • Fraunces serif headlines, editorial pacing
  • Warm paper background, wheat-sunset hero photo
  • Calm stat band and a static mandi rate table
  • Generous whitespace, hairline rules, no clutter
×
09 · TERRACOTTA — THE DISSENT

What Terracotta lends

Two signatures, borrowed with restraint — not the whole wardrobe.

  • Clay #B0512A as the single accent colour
  • The arch: hero frame, doorway sign-in, role colonnade
  • The sun medallion, once — as the sign-in keystone
  • Earthen surface tones under Terra’s paper
Palette: paper · ink · clay Green for crop status only No top stripes or tickers No rotated stamps No sideways-scrolling rows One arch motif, repeated quietly Static gridlines only

Display · Fraunces — from Terra
Good earth. Fair trade. One grid.

Headlines, stat numerals and prices of record. Italic in clay for the one word that matters.

Interface · Public Sans + IBM Plex Mono
Reserve 480 kg of wheat in Charsadda

Body and controls in Public Sans; rates, quantities and labels in Plex Mono — the mandi-board voice, kept still.

Shape · The arch — from Terracotta
border-radius: 999px 999px 22px 22px

One radius token, applied to the hero frame, the sign-in doorway, the role colonnade and stat crowns. Everything else stays rectangular and quiet.

Motion · Restrained
Fade-and-rise on scroll, 600 ms, once

No marquees, no tickers, no drifting rows. Hover lifts of 3 px maximum. Fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
